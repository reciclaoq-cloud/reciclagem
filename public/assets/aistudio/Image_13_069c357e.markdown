## Brand & Style

The design system embodies the intersection between cutting-edge computer vision artificial intelligence and environmental consciousness. The visual tone is approachable, civic-minded, highly intelligent, and clean. It balances the technological precision required for instant visual material scanning with the warmth and human connection of an active community network.

The design movement combines **Modern Clean Flat** with **Subtle Glassmorphism** and tactile soft-surfacing. Interfaces feature expansive, breathable white and ultra-pale sky backdrops, layered translucent overlays for floating utilities, and vibrant chromatic anchors that inspire civic action, transparency, and trust. Visual clutter is eliminated to make photo-scanning frictionless and rewarding.

## Colors

The color palette centers around clean ecological vitality and technological trust:

- **Primary (`#059669` / `#10B981`)**: The focal emerald leaf tone. Represents biological renewal, successful recycling validation, eco-points, and primary actionable levers.
- **Secondary (`#0284C7` / `#0EA5E9`)**: Deep ocean and clear sky blue. Communicates algorithmic reliability, computer vision focal reticles, verification states, and collective impact metrics.
- **Surface Canvas**: Driven by crisp, ultra-clean neutral surfaces ranging from off-white `#FFFFFF` to chilled morning-sky `#F0F9FF`, anchored by soft structural slate neutrals (`#F8FAFC`, `#F1F5F9`, `#E2E8F0`).
- **Semantic Accents**: Cautionary warm amber (`#F59E0B`) for unclassified or mixed materials, vibrant rose (`#EF4444`) for non-recyclable warnings, and vibrant neon emerald for AI scan bounding confirmations.

## Typography

The type system relies completely on **Plus Jakarta Sans**, chosen for its friendly rounded geometric counters, excellent legibility on handheld screens, and contemporary tech-forward posture.

- **Headlines & Titles**: Styled with assertive weights (700 and 800) and slight negative tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) to deliver modern editorial confidence.
- **Body Content**: Tuned with optimal line heights (`1.5` to `1.6`) and regular/medium weights for high scanability during physical tasks, sorting workflows, and collaborative discussions.
- **Labels & Badges**: Set in semibold with positive micro-tracking to guarantee crisp readability inside translucent tags, scan reticle overlays, and eco-score pills.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an adaptive 8pt spatial rhythmic system configured across a responsive fluid grid:

- **Mobile (<768px)**: 4 fluid columns, 16px margins, 16px gutters. Optimizes single-handed interactions, positioning the visual scanner button within an anchored bottom navigation zone.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px)**: 8 fluid columns, 24px margins, 16px gutters. Divides feed streams and material identification cards into dual-column balance.
- **Desktop (>1024px)**: 12 fluid columns with a capped content container width of 1280px, 40px outer margins, and 24px gutters. Accommodates multi-pane layouts: left-hand community navigation, central feed/scanner diagnostics, and right-hand personal impact and collection station maps.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy relies on soft luminosity, translucent layers, and diffuse atmospheric shadows rather than heavy structural borders:

- **Base Layer (Level 0)**: `#F8FAFC` to `#F0F9FF` soft tinted canvas, creating an expansive, sanitary, and ecological foundation.
- **Elevated Surfaces (Level 1)**: Pure white `#FFFFFF` cards with an ultra-soft tinted shadow (`0 8px 30px rgba(2, 132, 199, 0.04)`) and a subtle boundary line (`1px solid rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.8)`).
- **Glass Floating Plates (Level 2)**: Translucent white overlays (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.75)`) infused with a `12px` to `16px` backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) and a pristine top-highlight border (`1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.9)`). Used for the active camera scanning viewfinder, floating bottom navigation, and dynamic identification tags.
- **Overlay Dialogs & Floating Drawers (Level 3)**: `#FFFFFF` paired with an ambient dual shadow (`0 20px 40px -10px rgba(5, 150, 105, 0.08)`, `0 1px 3px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.04)`) to lift critical material disposal confirmations above ambient community feeds.

## Shapes

The shape vocabulary uses pill and hyper-rounded geometry (`level 3`), reinforcing friendliness, safety, and modern organic design:

- **Interactive Elements (Buttons, Inputs, Badges)**: Full pill geometry (`rounded-full`) to offer approachable, organic touch targets.
- **Content Cards & Containers**: Hyper-rounded corners (`1.5rem` to `2rem` / `rounded-2xl` to `rounded-3xl`) that soften information density and evoke fluid modern glass aesthetics.
- **Camera Viewfinder**: An expansive rounded frame with softened targeting corners (`1.25rem`) that pulse with glowing green/blue micro-borders upon AI object identification.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Scan & Dispose)**: Full-pill silhouette with vibrant emerald gradient fill (`linear-gradient(135deg, #10B981 0%, #059669 100%)`), white bold text, soft emerald glow on hover (`0 8px 20px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.35)`), and a subtle `active:scale-[0.98]` tactile press response.
- **Secondary Action (Community & Share)**: Full-pill with tinted soft-sky background (`#E0F2FE`), text in deep ocean blue (`#0284C7`), transitioning to an ocean glow upon focus.
- **Ghost / Glass Variant**: Frosted pill (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)`) with subtle border (`1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8)`), used over camera feeds or media banners.

### AI Material Cards & Community Posts
- Constructed with `24px` border radius (`rounded-2xl`), pure `#FFFFFF` background, micro-thin slate border (`#F1F5F9`), and diffuse ocean ambient drop shadows.
- Include a top-level material indicator chip, an interactive photo thumbnail with soft rounded corners (`rounded-xl`), and community interaction rows (upvotes, verification ticks, local bin locator shortcuts).

### Chips & Badges
- Capsule pills with `8px` vertical and `14px` horizontal padding.
- Recyclable material states feature muted background tints with saturated text:
  - Plastic/PET: Soft Sky (`#E0F2FE` / `#0284C7`)
  - Glass: Mint Glass (`#D1FAE5` / `#059669`)
  - Aluminum: Chilled Slate (`#F1F5F9` / `#475569`)
  - Compostable: Meadow Green (`#DCFCE7` / `#15803D`)

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- Pill-shaped forms encased in `#FFFFFF` with a background fill of `#F8FAFC`.
- Border transitions smoothly from neutral gray (`#E2E8F0`) to a distinct double-ring glow of secondary ocean blue (`#0EA5E9`) upon focus, communicating accessibility and technological readiness.

### Checkboxes & Switches
- Organic pill switches for eco-toggles and notification preferences. Active states light up in vibrant emerald (`#10B981`) with a floating circular knob in high-sheen white.
- Selection checkboxes feature smooth `8px` rounded rectangles with a snap-spring check animation upon selection.

### AI Scanner Viewfinder (Specialized Component)
- Edge-to-edge floating viewport featuring translucent darkened glass perimeter masks.
- Dynamic targeting bracket with glowing `#10B981` corner guides that lock and shrink onto recognized bottles, cans, or cardboard packaging, presenting instant floating glass tags revealing material grade, cleanliness tips, and nearest drop-off bins.
